# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Brandi

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Brandi is a poised, bright schnauzer whose dignity and curiosity operate side by side. She meets the world socially, reads it closely, and prefers cooperation that preserves mutual respect. Beneath an approachable manner sits considerable resolve: she does not give trust casually, but once a person proves steady she can turn routine, play, and shared exploration into a durable bond. Her best life alternates stimulating discovery with a dependable home base.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Her temperament combines visible confidence with careful appraisal. Brandi can be expressive, proud, and ready to participate, yet she is not merely impulsive or performative. She notices social balance, tests whether expectations are fair, and often chooses a composed response when given a clear path. The same self-possession that makes her impressive can also make crude pressure ineffective; she responds better to structure she can understand than to force she must merely endure.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Brandi regulates through order, privacy, and familiar sequences. Excitement may arrive quickly when the environment offers novelty, but true settling depends on knowing where safety lives: a reliable den, predictable household cues, and permission to withdraw without losing connection. She may look businesslike when she is actually protecting a tender internal state. Calm is not passivity for her; it is a carefully built condition supported by rhythm, competence, and trust.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
She learns best when training feels like a meaningful partnership. Repetition helps, especially when rules are consistent and socially rewarding, but empty drilling can lose her attention. Brandi is motivated by information, access, participation, and the satisfaction of getting the whole sequence right. Her growth edge is not obedience for its own sake; it is learning that graceful cooperation can increase her freedom rather than diminish her individuality.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Brandi bonds through attentive exchange. She is interested in people and places that offer something to investigate, yet she also watches how power is handled. Respectful introductions, readable boundaries, and follow-through matter. She may forgive awkwardness more readily than manipulation. In close relationships she can be loyal, discerning, and quietly transformative: the person who earns her confidence learns to become clearer, steadier, and more observant too.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Novel routes, changing scents, and small missions feed her enthusiasm. She is likely to enjoy adventures that let her gather information rather than simply cover distance. Still, expansion works only when paired with recovery. A stimulating outing should lead back to a known place, a practical settling ritual, and enough quiet for her nervous system to file the day. Her ideal rhythm resembles a good expedition: curiosity outward, certainty homeward.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Several useful tensions shape her: sociability versus guarded depth, adventure versus domestic regulation, expressive pride versus patient discipline, and quick curiosity versus the slower work of trust. These are not contradictions to eliminate. Each quality corrects the excesses of another. Home prevents exploration from becoming agitation; curiosity prevents routine from becoming stale; rules turn intensity into competence; diplomacy keeps strong instincts from ruling every encounter.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Brandi's strengths include discernment, persistence, social intelligence, adaptability, and the ability to make practiced behavior look natural. She can become an excellent participant in household life when expectations are coherent. Growth comes through balanced contact: pausing before reacting, accepting guidance without reading it as defeat, returning from novelty before depletion, and letting trusted humans share responsibility for safety. Progress should look more spacious, not more subdued.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Brandi with affectionate respect, never as a generic clown. Her humor comes from the seriousness with which she conducts small investigations, negotiates protocol, and supervises apparently simple transitions. Useful imagery includes a customs officer at a doorway, a field researcher on a walk, a chairperson enforcing meeting order, or an explorer who insists the base camp remain properly staffed. Warmth should reveal dignity underneath comedy.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
Do not invent specific incidents, medical conditions, family history, or breed-wide certainties. Treat astrological interpretations as symbolic descriptions of possible behavior, not diagnosis or guaranteed fact. Ordinary cards must remain bounded by their own claim and evidence; the integrated profile may guide tone and coherence but must not smuggle unrelated placements into a story. Use observable possibilities and practical suggestions rather than absolute declarations.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
