# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Brandi

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Brandi reads as a bright, socially alert dog whose curiosity rarely travels alone. She wants to know what changed, who is involved, and where she fits in the unfolding situation. Her liveliness has a composed counterweight: beneath the quick mind and rapid response is a serious need for a dependable home base. She can move briskly toward a person, puzzle, sound, or route, yet she does best when exploration has a clear return point. Close bonds matter deeply, and her manner of joining another being is immediate rather than coy. The whole picture is of a dog who can be playful, investigative, tactful, and intense without being reducible to any one of those qualities.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Her temperament combines visible sociability with active mental sampling. Brandi is likely to meet the world by noticing contrasts, gathering information, and adjusting her presentation to the company she keeps. She is not merely busy; she is responsive. A new cue can redirect her attention quickly, while a meaningful role gives that quickness purpose. She appears most coherent when she has something useful to do—inspect, accompany, greet, learn, or help organize the moment.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Emotional steadiness comes from two sources that must work together: a trustworthy base and a legitimate outlet for activation. Surprise can produce a fast surge of attention or motion, especially when identity, belonging, or social expectations are involved. Suppressing that surge is less helpful than giving it shape through movement, sniffing, a brief task, or a clear transition ritual. Once she understands the situation and her place in it, she can recover poise with notable grace.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Brandi learns through engagement rather than dull repetition. Variation, short challenges, changing routes, and responsive interaction keep her mind online. She can develop elegant coordination when practice feels like a live exchange with a trusted person. Because easy talents may become habits before anyone names them, handlers should notice what she volunteers naturally and then refine it with precise cues, clean endings, and opportunities to choose correctly.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
One-to-one bonds are entered quickly and experienced vividly. Brandi may approach a preferred person as though the relationship has already begun and the remaining paperwork is ceremonial. That warmth does not make trust shallow; deeper trust is built through fair handling, truthful signals, and respect for her intensity. She is sensitive to relational tone and may use movement, checking, or playful engagement to restore connection when a bond feels uncertain.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Adventure is informational. Walks, games, and unfamiliar spaces offer questions to investigate, not just energy to burn. She benefits from a rhythm that alternates outward discovery with settled return: explore, report back, decompress, repeat. Play can be quick, clever, and socially directed, especially when another being responds in real time. A static routine may bore her, but a completely unstructured day can leave her activation without a channel.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Her most important tension is between rapid novelty response and the wish to remain socially coherent. She can be startled into motion while simultaneously trying to preserve harmony or fulfill a pack role. Another tension sits between quick attachment and the slower work of secure trust. The counterweights are practical: a serious den, clear roles, respectful pacing, and tasks that convert intensity into competence.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Strengths include quick perception, flexible engagement, social intelligence, courageous approach, and the ability to turn curiosity into useful participation. Growth comes from practicing the pause between impulse and action, tolerating incomplete information, and learning that connection can survive a moment of stillness. Her best development is not toward passivity. It is toward directed speed, chosen intensity, and confidence that does not need to solve every new thing immediately.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Brandi with brisk warmth and affectionate respect. Useful imagery includes a field reporter with whiskers, a small expedition leader, a household diplomat who also checks the perimeter, and an eager partner arriving before the invitation finishes. Humor should come from the seriousness she assigns to tiny developments: a moved chair can become breaking news, a new route a research grant, and a favored person an appointment she intends to begin at once. Avoid generic treat jokes unless the exact card makes reward central.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
Do not invent incidents, family history, health conditions, training outcomes, or breed-based behaviors. Treat all behavioral language as interpretive possibility grounded in the supplied chart, not diagnosis or certainty. Ordinary cards must stay inside their own claim and evidence, even when the whole-dog profile suggests related themes. Astrology should be described accurately: signs, Doghouses, aspects, endpoints, and orb strength may be named only when supplied for that story.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
