# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Brandi

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Brandi reads as a composed little sentinel with a private interior and a surprisingly public sense of purpose. She is likely to notice the room before joining it, decide what belongs where, and then take up a useful post near the people she considers hers. Her chart combines warmth, watchfulness, investigative intensity, social tact, and sudden independent streaks. The coherent picture is not of a dog who simply obeys or rebels, but of one who wants participation to make sense: safety first, relationship second, assignment understood, then wholehearted engagement.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Her core temperament blends dignity with alert curiosity. Libra rising gives her an instinct for measured approaches and social calibration, while the Aries relationship axis brings briskness and candor into close encounters. Cancer at the Midheaven emphasizes protective contribution; Capricorn at the chart base adds seriousness about home, routine, and dependable structure. She may appear courteous at first and far more decisive once a bond or responsibility is established.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Brandi is best understood as a dog who regulates through orientation. She benefits from knowing where home base is, who is present, what changed, and what happens next. Atmosphere can reach her quickly, especially through local sounds, movement, and human tone, yet she is not merely passive or porous. Investigation, patrol, retreat, and renewed contact can all be ways of restoring equilibrium. Calm grows when she is allowed to gather information without being hurried into a performance.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Learning is strongest when it gives her a real function. She is likely to respond well to short, intelligible sequences, visible outcomes, and tasks that convert vigilance into skill: checking a threshold, finding an object, settling at a station, or returning after inspection. Novelty can be highly motivating when it involves resources, puzzles, scent, or an unusual problem, though repetition without meaning may lose her. Choice and structure work better together than either permissiveness or pressure alone.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Trust is active rather than automatic. Brandi may negotiate closeness by approaching, pausing, reading the other party, and deciding how much contact fits. Once committed, she can make the relationship central to her behavior and may expect a partner to be clear, brave, and consistent. She does not need every interaction softened; she needs it made legible. Respecting a small hesitation often produces more devotion than insisting on instant sociability.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Her play has an investigative edge. Food toys, bones, hidden objects, new smells, and odd household developments can become serious research projects. Adventure is likely to begin with reconnaissance rather than reckless launch, and familiar territory may be continually reread for fresh information. Daily rhythm should include a secure resting place, purposeful engagement, and room for a few self-appointed inspections. The comic contrast is a compact dog behaving as though local conditions require a full intelligence service.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Several chart polarities keep Brandi from becoming one-note. The private dog who values a sober den also wants a visible pack role. The socially graceful first approach can meet a much faster, more direct impulse in one-to-one bonds. Sensitivity to atmosphere is counterweighted by practical investigation; protectiveness is enlivened by novelty. These tensions are not flaws to eliminate. They are the mechanism by which she adjusts, chooses, and becomes more capable.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Her strengths include situational awareness, loyalty expressed through usefulness, persistence with interesting problems, and the ability to develop reliable routines around complex impulses. Growth comes from practicing transitions: observe and then return, guard and then stand down, hesitate and then choose, investigate and then release. Handlers can help by rewarding recovery and completion, not only the first alert or burst of effort. She is most impressive when intensity becomes an organized contribution.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Brandi with affectionate respect. Her humor should come from exact contrasts: diplomatic manners attached to firm opinions, a tiny patrol officer auditing a hallway, or a resource puzzle treated like a sealed criminal case. Favor tactile domestic images, thresholds, listening pauses, strategic seating, scent trails, and the solemn presentation of toys. Avoid making her frantic, mystical, bossy in a generic way, or cute at the expense of dignity.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
The astrology is playful interpretation, not diagnosis, veterinary advice, or a substitute for observation. Do not claim breed-based behavior, medical conditions, trauma history, or biographical events not supplied in the workspace. Present tendencies as possibilities a handler may recognize. Ordinary cards must stay within their own evidence even when the whole-dog profile supplies consistent voice and context.
