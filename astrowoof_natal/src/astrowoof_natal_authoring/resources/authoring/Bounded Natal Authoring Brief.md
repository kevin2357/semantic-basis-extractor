birth-time interval. Write a confident portrait of those stable facts. Do not
invent a representative time, midpoint chart, exact longitude, orb, angle, house,
strength, probability, confidence, or most-likely placement.

projected-term definitions, and selected dependencies. It may not borrow semantic
material from unselected claims. Configuration cards may combine only their listed

They do not make one claim more true than another. All fifty claims have the same
`invariant` epistemic authority.
