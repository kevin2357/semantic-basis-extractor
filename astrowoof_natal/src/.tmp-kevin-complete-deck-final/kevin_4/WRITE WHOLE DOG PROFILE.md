# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin meets life as a friendly original: socially attentive, visibly warm, and capable of sudden independent turns when curiosity catches fire. Beneath the Golden Retriever welcome is a serious trust system. He does not merely enjoy people, play, and exploration; he invests in them, tests their reliability, and becomes more expressive when the bond feels secure. His chart suggests a dog whose charm and intensity belong together rather than canceling each other out.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
His outward style is inventive and a little unpredictable, while a strong Libra emphasis seeks fairness, cooperation, and relational balance. Kevin can look easygoing without being casual. He notices how interactions are arranged, who is included, and whether expectations feel mutually intelligible. Confidence grows fastest when he can participate rather than simply be managed.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
A Pisces Moon in the first Doghouse points to a body that may register atmosphere quickly. Calm voices, coherent routines, and unhurried transitions can help him settle, especially after stimulation. Emotional permeability does not mean fragility; it means the room can enter through his senses before anyone has explained what is happening.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Kevin learns through relationship, meaningful action, and the chance to investigate. He is likely to engage best when training has a social purpose, a clear outlet, and enough novelty to prevent mental flatness. Repetition can build skill, but mechanical drilling may lose him; small variations and opportunities to choose can preserve both precision and enthusiasm.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
One-to-one bonds are central, dramatic, and developmental for Kevin. He may seek visible engagement from a favorite person while also resisting the feeling of being overdirected. Trust deepens when a human can remain present without gripping tighter, giving him a dependable counterpart rather than an audience, jailer, or vending machine.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play is not separate from attachment for Kevin. Chase, movement, exploration, and shared missions can become ways of confirming the bond. He may alternate between companionable participation and an investigative detour, then return with the satisfaction of a dog who has completed unofficial fieldwork.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
His major counterweight is closeness versus freedom: he wants partnership, yet novelty can interrupt the partnership on its own schedule. Another tension pairs social harmony with deep intensity; he may smooth the surface while caring strongly underneath. Structure helps, provided it channels energy instead of flattening personality.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin's strengths include relational intelligence, recoverable enthusiasm, investigative courage, and the ability to turn strong drives into cooperative work. Growth lies in practicing transitions, tolerating incomplete information, and learning that independence need not require disconnection. His humans can support this by rewarding check-ins, creating safe outlets, and treating trust as a skill built in motion.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin with affection, dignity, and dry observational humor. Useful images include a cheerful detective, a social diplomat carrying muddy evidence, an explorer who keeps one ear tuned to home, or a dreamer accidentally promoted to public relations. Avoid making him a generic sunny retriever; his warmth has depth, strategy, and occasional plot twists.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
