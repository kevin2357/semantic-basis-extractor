## Practical Guidance

### Do 1

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: dos.0 -->
Use specific praise—“good hold” or “good settle”—instead of undirected excitement.
<!-- END FIELD: dos.0 -->

### Do 2

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: dos.1 -->
Alternate animated repetitions with brief calm resets.
<!-- END FIELD: dos.1 -->

### Do 3

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: dos.2 -->
Give him occasional jobs with a clear finish he can feel proud of.
<!-- END FIELD: dos.2 -->

### Don't 1

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: donts.0 -->

<!-- END FIELD: donts.0 -->

### Don't 2

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: donts.1 -->

<!-- END FIELD: donts.1 -->

### Don't 3

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: donts.2 -->
