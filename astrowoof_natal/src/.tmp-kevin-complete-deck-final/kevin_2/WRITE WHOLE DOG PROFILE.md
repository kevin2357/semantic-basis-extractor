# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a socially attentive Golden Retriever whose friendliness carries more depth than breezy charm suggests. He meets life by reading the room, testing the edges, and locating the person or place that feels trustworthy. Adventure matters, but so does a dependable return point. His strongest patterns combine diplomacy with intensity: he may look easygoing while privately tracking tone, sequence, access, and whether everyone is still connected.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
His baseline is warm, responsive, and relationship-minded, with a lively streak that resists being managed by blunt force. Kevin often negotiates through body language before he commits. He can be playful and visible, yet he also wants agency over approach, retreat, and timing. The coherent center is not simple compliance; it is cooperative participation built on respect.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin appears highly receptive to atmosphere. Emotional steadiness comes from familiar rhythms, gentle transitions, and enough information to understand what is changing. When pressure arrives too abruptly, he may need to move, investigate, or briefly withdraw before he can reorganize. Calm handling works best when it gives his sensitivity a practical route rather than demanding instant suppression.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
He learns through a blend of curiosity, social feedback, repetition, and meaningful stakes. Novelty can energize him, especially when discovery becomes a shared game, but intensity may narrow his attention when trust or access feels uncertain. Training is strongest when rules are consistent, choices are real, and rewards support—not replace—the relationship. Short, clear practice helps him convert instinct into dependable skill.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
One-to-one bonds carry unusual weight for Kevin. He is likely to notice micro-signals in a favorite person and to care deeply about repair after disconnection. Trust is not merely affection; it is permission to relax vigilance. He may alternate between seeking closeness and preserving room to act independently. A reliable human becomes both companion and return address.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play can be theatrical, physical, and contagious, especially when imagination and pursuit meet. Exploration expands him, yet a secure base makes that expansion possible. Daily life benefits from alternating outward activity with private decompression: sniffing, movement, social engagement, then a quieter review of the territory. His rhythm is less a straight line than a loop—depart, discover, check back, settle.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Several useful contradictions shape him: harmony versus fierce preference, freedom versus attachment, porous sensitivity versus strong control, and novelty versus the need for a known base. These are not flaws to eliminate. Each side regulates the other. Connection keeps independence from becoming isolation; curiosity keeps caution from becoming stagnation; structure gives emotional permeability a container.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin’s gifts include social intelligence, resilient curiosity, deep loyalty, imaginative action, and the ability to build strong habits when his whole system agrees. Growth asks for graceful recalibration: tolerating imperfect transitions, practicing impulse control without humiliation, and learning that boundaries can protect a bond rather than threaten it. His best development turns intensity into discernment and excitement into skilled participation.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write him with affectionate intelligence rather than baby talk. The voice may be wry, tender, observational, or brisk, but it should preserve his dignity. Useful imagery includes a host checking the guest list, a detective revisiting one clue, a camper who needs a marked trail home, or an actor waiting for the correct entrance. Humor should come from the seriousness Kevin assigns to small relational or environmental details.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
