# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin reads as a bright, socially attuned dog with an unusual streak: he wants closeness, but not at the price of becoming predictable. He meets the world with visible originality, seeks a favorite human’s attention with warmth and flair, and often turns action, play, and relationship into one combined event. Beneath that approachable surface is a more private intensity around trust. He notices atmosphere, studies what matters, and may need both harmony and honest depth before he fully relaxes. His best life balances secure rituals with novelty, shared adventures with decompression, and clear training with room for personality.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
His outward temperament is inventive, friendly, and a little unconventional. He is likely to make an entrance in his own way, improvise around routines, and recruit people into whatever has captured his interest. Libra emphasis adds social awareness and a preference for workable peace; Sagittarius and Aquarius signatures add range, humor, and appetite for the new. He can therefore seem easygoing while quietly insisting on freedom, fairness, and meaningful engagement.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin appears highly responsive to emotional weather. Comfort is not only a bed or familiar room; it is the felt tone of the household. He may absorb tension, drift into a soft dreamlike state when safe, or become more active when the atmosphere is unsettled. Regulation works best through calm presence, predictable care, sensory decompression, and simple transitions rather than pressure. Deep trust grows when his signals are respected before they become dramatic.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
He learns through relationship, curiosity, repetition, and visible payoff. Novelty can switch on intense investigation, while play and attention can make practice feel irresistible. He benefits from clear sequences and stable rules, but rigid drilling may collide with his experimental side. Short sessions, meaningful rewards, gradual difficulty, and chances to solve rather than merely comply are likely to reveal his strongest coordination.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Bonding is central, expressive, and layered. Kevin may seek a primary person openly, yet still need agency inside the bond. He is capable of deep loyalty and close observation, especially where trust feels vulnerable. Social harmony matters to him, but harmony should not be mistaken for shallowness: he may watch, test, or revisit an interaction until he understands whether the relationship is truly safe. Repair, consistency, and invitations work better than emotional force.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play is both joy and communication. Kevin may turn training, greetings, exploration, and attention into a theatrical shared activity. Adventure broadens him, especially when it is undertaken with trusted company, while home rituals provide the secure base that makes roaming fun. A satisfying rhythm alternates sniffing and discovery, animated play, cooperative tasks, and real rest. Too little novelty can flatten him; too much stimulation without recovery can scatter him.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
The chart repeatedly sets freedom against attachment, novelty against rules, and intensity against social smoothness. Kevin may want to be near his person and simultaneously choose the route, pace, or method. He may seek peace while privately fixating on a cue that does not make sense. These are not contradictions to eliminate. They are counterweights to coordinate: offer structure without micromanagement, closeness without crowding, and exploration without abandoning the secure base.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Strengths include social intelligence, inventive engagement, resilient curiosity, expressive play, deep loyalty, and the ability to build useful coordination through practice. Growth edges include tolerating delayed gratification, leaving an investigation unfinished, accepting routine without turning it into a negotiation, and communicating discomfort before intensity rises. His development is less about becoming more obedient in the abstract and more about discovering that dependable structure can protect both freedom and trust.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin with affectionate dignity and lively specificity. His humor belongs in the gap between his golden public relations department and his private investigative bureau: a charming greeter who may also audit the emotional paperwork. Favor scenes of doorway improvisation, ceremonial toy delivery, purposeful sniffing, adventurous detours, meaningful pauses, and negotiations conducted with one eloquent look. Keep him warm, clever, embodied, and recognizably canine rather than turning him into a human in fur.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
