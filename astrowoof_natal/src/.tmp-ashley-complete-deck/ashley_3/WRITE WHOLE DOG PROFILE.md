# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Ashley

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Ashley comes across as a bright, observant poodle whose sensitivity is active rather than fragile. She studies changes in people, rooms, routes, and routines, then decides how to participate. Her strongest pattern is thoughtful engagement: affection matters, novelty interests her, and structure helps her convert both into confident action. She is not a blankly agreeable dog. She has preferences, notices inconsistencies, and tends to offer her fullest cooperation when the situation makes emotional and practical sense.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Her baseline combines curiosity with deliberation. Ashley can be lively and inventive, yet she rarely feels careless; even enthusiasm tends to include a quick assessment of safety, meaning, or sequence. She may look composed while conducting a remarkably detailed internal review. Once she understands the pattern, she can become steady, capable, and surprisingly original inside it.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Comfort is physical, rhythmic, and relational for Ashley. Familiar resting places, repeatable transitions, patient handling, and a calm household atmosphere help her settle. She may absorb tension before anyone addresses it directly, and uncertain signals can linger in her system. Regulation improves when a person makes the next step plain without crowding her response.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Ashley learns by connecting cues to context, not by collecting commands in isolation. She is good at noticing tiny changes and may invent an answer before the handler realizes a question was asked. Durable learning comes through consistent sequences, useful jobs, and enough repetition to make the rule trustworthy. Motivation is strongest when training preserves dignity and gives her intelligence somewhere concrete to go.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Trust is built through accurate reading on both sides. Ashley is affectionate, but closeness does not erase her need for privacy, pacing, or choice. She may seek company and then relocate a few feet away, or check a person carefully before relaxing into warmth. The bond deepens when her handler treats these adjustments as communication rather than rejection.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play and exploration suit Ashley best when they have shape. She enjoys novelty, investigation, and the occasional burst of inspired weirdness, but she also values a reliable return path and a clear ending. New territory can become a project: inspect, compare, test, then proceed. Daily life benefits from alternating stimulation with unhurried recovery.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Several useful tensions organize her character: imagination can blur a practical cue; social interest can compete with the wish for a protected den; and independence can appear inside a strong desire to cooperate. Rules help, but rigid pressure can make the conflict louder. Her counterweights are time, consistency, sensory clarity, and a handler willing to notice when two valid needs are active together.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Ashley’s strengths include pattern recognition, persistence, emotional attunement, and the ability to make a routine her own. She can turn careful observation into dependable behavior and bring originality to familiar tasks. Growth involves distinguishing atmosphere from instruction, tolerating small uncertainties, and practicing transitions until choice and structure stop feeling like rivals.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Ashley with warmth, intelligence, and a slightly exacting comic dignity. Her humor lives in the seriousness with which she may inspect a minor change, negotiate a resting arrangement, or improve an assignment nobody knew had design flaws. Useful images include a quiet detective, a thoughtful stage manager, a cartographer of household moods, and an adventurer who checks the return address before departure. Avoid making her fussy, mystical, or generically clever.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->

Treat astrological language as interpretive rather than deterministic. Ordinary stories must stay within their own supplied placements, aspects, and projected evidence; this profile provides continuity of character, not permission to import unrelated chart facts.
