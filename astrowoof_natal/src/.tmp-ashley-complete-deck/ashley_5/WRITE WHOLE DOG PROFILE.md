# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Ashley

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Ashley is a perceptive, relationship-oriented poodle whose curiosity and caution often arrive together. She meets the world by investigating it, yet her deepest ease comes from familiar bodies, places, and rituals. She can look socially capable and even purposeful while privately absorbing atmosphere and checking whether the pack is secure. Her chart repeatedly joins public usefulness, close bonding, exploratory learning, and a strong need for a dependable den. The result is not a simple “bold” or “sensitive” dog, but a dog who becomes brave through attachment, observant through uncertainty, and effective when her careful noticing is given a constructive job.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Her baseline is alert, intelligent, and quietly tenacious. She does not merely notice a new situation; she studies its seams, motives, exits, and emotional temperature. Taurus signatures add steadiness, bodily preference, and loyalty to what has proven comfortable, while Gemini and Virgo themes keep her mind active and discriminating. Ashley may therefore alternate between intense scrutiny and an almost immovable commitment to the chosen bed, person, route, or method. She is adaptable in thought, selective in trust, and more persistent than her social grace first suggests.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Ashley regulates through sensory continuity: a known resting place, a familiar companion, an expected sequence, and play that lets the body complete what the nerves have started. She is porous to mood and may register tension before anyone names it. Retreat is not failure for her; it is part of how she restores coherence. At the same time, comfort can become sticky if change arrives too abruptly. The best regulation gives her both an anchor and a small, manageable path back into engagement.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
She learns best when investigation has a purpose. Repetition alone may bore the questioning part of her, while novelty without structure can flood the cautious part. Productive learning lets her inspect, compare, practice, and then demonstrate a useful result. Adventure is especially developmental when it includes clear landmarks, solvable tasks, and time to gather information. Her motivation strengthens when competence benefits the relationship or earns a visible place in the pack’s activity.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Ashley bonds deeply and selectively. Companionship is not background decoration; it is a stabilizing interface through which she understands unfamiliar events. She may mirror a trusted person, seek proximity without constant fuss, and test whether closeness remains dependable when circumstances change. Trust grows through consistency, respectful pacing, and honest signals. Forced sociability or sudden emotional pressure can make her more investigative, not more open. Once secure, she can use that bond as a bridge toward new people, places, and responsibilities.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Her play has weight, rhythm, and a practical bodily function. She may prefer games that build momentum gradually, reward persistence, or recur in a beloved form. Adventure appeals to her when it becomes an expedition rather than chaos: there should be something to examine, a route to understand, and a companion worth checking back with. Daily life works well when vigorous moments alternate with committed rest. Ashley’s ideal rhythm resembles fieldwork followed by an excellent nap in the officially approved location.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Several chart tensions ask her to balance home and visibility, attachment and autonomy, action and caution, comfort and development. Her first response can be more intense than her lighter, curious identity ultimately wants to remain. Her drive may push forward while her presentation pauses to assess. Her longing for familiar security can conflict with the very adventures that help her grow. These are not contradictions to eliminate. They are paired engines that work best when transitions are gradual and each side receives a legitimate role.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Ashley’s strengths include close observation, durable loyalty, social tact, embodied persistence, and the ability to turn sensitivity into useful awareness. She can become an excellent checker, learner, companion, and quiet contributor when expectations are legible. Growth involves acting before every uncertainty is solved, tolerating small variations in beloved routines, and discovering that safety can travel with her rather than exist only in one place. Her best development is incremental, relational, and concrete—not a demand that she stop being careful.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Ashley with warmth, intelligence, and understated comedy. Her humor lives in disproportionate seriousness about small investigations, the solemn defense of a preferred resting spot, and the way she can conduct a full inquiry before agreeing that nothing happened. Useful imagery includes a customs inspector, field researcher, careful host, loyal archivist, trail surveyor, or velvet-robed security consultant. Avoid making her merely anxious, fussy, or precious; the joke should preserve her dignity and reveal the logic inside her behavior.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
