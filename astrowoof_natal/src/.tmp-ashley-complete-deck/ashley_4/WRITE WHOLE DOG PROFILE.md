# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Ashley

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Ashley is an alert, relationship-centered poodle whose intelligence works through close observation, emotional weather-reading, and an active sense of usefulness. She can be sociable and bright while still keeping private depths, sudden independent reactions, and exacting standards about trust. Her best self appears when she has a meaningful role, a responsive favorite person, room to investigate, and humane structure rather than blunt control.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Quick-minded, curious, and highly responsive, Ashley tends to gather information before settling on a response. She is neither simply outgoing nor simply reserved: she engages through selective attention, tests the social atmosphere, and becomes animated when there is something to understand or coordinate. A composed exterior may conceal intense internal sorting.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Ashley appears porous to tone and atmosphere. Changes in a room, uncertainty in a person, or ambiguous signals may reach her before anyone has named them. Regulation is helped by a dependable home base, clear transitions, and permission to pause. When overloaded, she may investigate, withdraw, fixate, or produce an abrupt burst of unconventional behavior rather than offer a simple, readable complaint.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
She learns best when information is precise, varied, and connected to a real purpose. Repetition without meaning may lose her, while small puzzles, clear cues, and opportunities to participate can hold her attention. Patience and structure matter, but so does dialogue: Ashley is motivated by understanding the assignment and by feeling that her response changes what happens next.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Primary bonds are central to Ashley's identity. She is attentive to reciprocity, fairness, and the reliability of signals within her pack. Trust can deepen powerfully, yet it may carry vigilance: she notices inconsistencies and may protect access to vulnerable feelings. Gentle repair, predictable follow-through, and respectful inclusion strengthen her more than forced sociability.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Ashley combines exploratory curiosity with a need to return to a secure baseline. Novel scents, routes, people, and games can enliven her, especially when she has time to inspect rather than being rushed into participation. Her daily rhythm benefits from alternating engagement with decompression: investigate, connect, retreat, then rejoin on her own terms.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Her chart repeatedly pairs sensitivity with intensity, sociability with scrutiny, and independence with strong attachment. The same intelligence that makes her adaptable can make mixed messages unusually costly. Her wish to help may compete with her wish to bond casually; her appetite for novelty may arise from hidden unease as readily as delight. Counterweights are calm clarity, bounded choices, and trusted routines that remain flexible.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Ashley’s strengths include perceptiveness, social intelligence, loyalty, inventiveness, and the capacity to turn subtle information into useful action. Growth comes from learning that not every atmospheric shift requires investigation, not every uncertainty threatens trust, and not every role must be performed perfectly. Supported well, she can transform vigilance into discernment and intensity into devoted cooperation.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Ashley with warmth, dignity, and a dry glint of comedy. Useful images include a small inspector making rounds, a radio catching stations others miss, a detective pausing over one displaced object, or a hostess who also audits the guest list. Avoid making her merely fussy, mystical, or controlling; the humor should reveal how seriously she takes subtle jobs and social signals.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
Do not invent events from Ashley's life, medical or behavioral diagnoses, breed-based guarantees, or claims beyond the supplied astrology. Describe possibilities and recognizable patterns rather than certainties. Ordinary cards must remain bounded by their own claim and evidence; the profile may unify tone and characterization but cannot import unrelated placements or aspects.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
