### Handler Story

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: no_astro.body.handler -->

Teach a small sequence she can perform when change enters the house: orient to you, move to a station, wait for information, receive release. Rehearsed this way, each interruption becomes evidence that she can meet surprise without being swallowed by it.
