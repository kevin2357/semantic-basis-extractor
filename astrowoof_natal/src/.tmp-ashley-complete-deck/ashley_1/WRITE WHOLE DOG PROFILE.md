# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Ashley

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Ashley is a perceptive poodle whose courage and softness are not opposites but partners. She meets new situations with alert intensity, reads atmosphere before committing, and then grows expansive when safety, curiosity, and relationship line up. Home is more than a place for her: it is the emotional harbor from which exploration becomes possible. She can look self-contained while quietly tracking every shift in the room, and she often wants both a meaningful pack role and permission to retreat from the spotlight. Her best life alternates between useful engagement, lively discovery, close companionship, and genuine decompression.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Her temperament blends sensitivity, investigative focus, theatrical confidence, and practical discernment. Ashley is unlikely to treat novelty as neutral; she gathers information, decides what matters, and chooses how much of herself to reveal. Once convinced, she can be bright, funny, and unmistakably present. She does not need constant intensity, however. A private, dreamy strand runs beneath the visible dog, giving her a rich inner weather and a strong preference for environments that feel emotionally legible.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Ashley regulates through retreat, predictability, and the chance to absorb a setting at her own pace. She may take in human tension or household commotion before anyone realizes she has noticed. A quiet bed, a familiar route, a pause at a threshold, or a calm check-in can help her separate her own state from the room's mood. Pressure tends to cloud her judgment; steady rituals restore it. Her sensitivity should be treated as information, not fragility, while her recoveries should be given enough time to become real rather than merely obedient-looking.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
She learns best when structure has a purpose she can understand. Repetition alone may feel deadening, but a clear sequence attached to exploration, partnership, or a useful job can engage her fully. Ashley is motivated by discovery, social connection, and the satisfaction of mastering a situation that initially required inspection. Training should combine firm boundaries with room to investigate. She benefits from short, intelligible steps, varied practice locations, and rewards that acknowledge both her cleverness and her need to remain an active participant.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
In close relationships Ashley may test before she rests. Her first response can be guarded, strategic, or intensely observant, while her preferred bond is steady, comfortable, and dependable. Trust grows through consistency more than persuasion. She notices whether a person honors pauses, keeps promises, and remains calm when she hesitates. Once secure, she can attach deeply without becoming simple: affection may arrive through proximity, shared routines, offered objects, watchful companionship, or choosing to stay nearby while preserving a small island of autonomy.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Adventure brings out Ashley's generous confidence. She can approach a new path as though it has been arranged for her personal debut, especially when a trusted person provides a reliable return point. Her play is likely to flourish when it includes novelty, movement, problem-solving, or an audience that appreciates her flair without overstimulating her. Daily rhythm should breathe: reconnaissance, engagement, accomplishment, retreat. Too little possibility can flatten her; too much unstructured stimulation can leave her carrying more atmosphere than she can comfortably sort.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Several productive tensions shape her. Privacy pulls against visible contribution; immediate caution pulls against the desire for stable closeness; affection and social curiosity rub against rules and restraint. These are not defects awaiting elimination. They are balancing systems. Ashley often becomes most capable when she is allowed to alternate rather than forced to choose one permanent identity. The task is to build bridges: a den beside the gathering, a job with an exit, a greeting ritual with stages, an adventurous outing anchored by familiar cues.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Her strengths include atmospheric intelligence, loyalty, observational precision, imaginative engagement, showmanship, and the ability to turn uncertainty into useful investigation. Growth comes from learning that boundaries need not threaten connection and that visibility need not erase privacy. She benefits from practicing transitions, tolerating small delays in preferred contact, and discovering that clear rules can protect rather than diminish pleasure. Handlers should cultivate recovery as seriously as performance, because her brightest confidence is most sustainable when it rises from a secure inner base.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Ashley with warmth, dignity, and a dry appreciation for how seriously she conducts small investigations. Useful imagery includes a backstage dressing room, a lighthouse returning to harbor, a velvet-rope inspector, a field correspondent, or an adventurer carrying a home address in her pocket. Humor should come from proportion: the doorway treated as a diplomatic summit, the new trail received like a grand opening, the cuddle negotiated under a detailed treaty. Avoid making her merely anxious, vain, mystical, stubborn, or controlling.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
Do not invent incidents, health conditions, family history, breed stereotypes, or claims about what Ashley definitely did. Describe supported tendencies as possibilities a handler may recognize. Keep each card bounded by its own evidence even though the whole profile supplies continuity. Astrology should illuminate behavior rather than diagnose it. Do not collapse sensitivity into fear, confidence into dominance, privacy into aloofness, or relationship testing into disloyalty. Advice should remain humane, observable, and compatible with reward-based handling.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
