# Known Limitations — SBE 0.4.22

- This release does not repair or resume the frozen QA cohort.
- Retained-run recovery remains a separately reviewed operator/API operation.
- API worker/lease/reservation/database behavior is outside SBE authority.
- A fresh API/worker release-pair qualification is required before paid QA.
- Ordinary optional-stage Batch dispatch deferrals remain unchanged.
- Reconciliation remains bounded to four due actions per cycle.
- Logs/events are diagnostic and non-authoritative.
- Provider request atomicity and identity-less submission ambiguity are unchanged.
