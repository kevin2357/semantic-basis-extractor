# SBE 0.4.28 Known Limitations

- The new terminal-review publication-before-exit boundary is qualified only for
  exact Natal interactive Responses. Batch and bounded route parity is deferred.
- A review-required result may retain unresolved provider custody. It is
  editorially terminal but not custody-final until supported reconciliation and
  providerless-denial follow-up complete.
- The result does not assert API reservations, capacity, leases, billing
  settlement, publication eligibility, or product status.
- `latest` native-result discovery is diagnostic only. API must ingest the exact
  result/receipt identity transported by the invocation-bound command envelope.
- The release does not decide whether the historical Pippin or Duchess editorial
  review outcome was substantively correct and does not repair or resume those
  retained workspaces.
