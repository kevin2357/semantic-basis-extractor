# Known Limitations — SBE 0.4.24

- Historical deterministic rebuild is intentionally limited to SBE 0.4.23
  exact-Natal interactive creative retries with complete compatible evidence.
- Historical bounded, Batch, polish, critic, candidate, or other request shapes are
  not reconstructed by this compatibility adapter.
- Missing or changed runtime, resources, profile, configuration, source, feedback,
  prompt, redaction, path containment, or digest evidence refuses before create.
- The provider submission/identity-persistence atomicity gap remains unchanged and
  fail-closed after provider call entry.
- SBE does not assert API-global reservation, capacity, lease, admission, billing,
  entitlement, or publication facts.
