Perform a hybrid-voice differentiation pass only. Preserve all locked fields, evidence, claim order, canonical claims, handler prose, direct-to-dog prose, humor, dos, and donts.

Hybrid mode must not read like handler advice with “you” added.

Use this functional distinction:

- Handler explains what may be happening inside Bre.
- Direct-to-dog expresses Bre’s imagined perspective.
- Hybrid describes the reciprocal interaction created by Bre and the person together.

Every hybrid body must include:

1. what Bre brings, signals, needs, or does;
2. what the person brings, signals, or does;
3. how those two contributions affect one another;
4. when appropriate, what Bre’s next response tells the person.

Prefer relational feedback loops over one-way instructions.

Strong hybrid patterns include:

- “Bre brings X; you bring Y; together Z becomes possible.”
- “Bre signals X; you answer with Y; her next response shows whether Z is available.”
- “You establish X; Bre experiments with Y; together you discover Z.”
- “When Bre does X, your Y changes the next step; when you do Y, Bre can respond with Z.”
- “The shared rhythm is X → Y → Z.”
- “Bre’s job is X; your job is Y; neither side has to do the other’s work.”

Do not mechanically reuse these sentence frames. They describe relational structures, not templates.

Avoid making hybrid bodies merely:

- commands directed at the handler;
- summaries of the handler body;
- “Bre’s placement means X, so you should Y”;
- generic advice followed by the word “together.”

No more than 25% of hybrid bodies may begin with:

- “Bre’s…”
- an astrological placement;
- an imperative such as Give, Use, Let, Keep, Make, Build, or Treat.

In light and full astrology, the astrological source may be named, but the body must still center the interaction rather than explain the placement.

Preserve current hybrid bodies that already establish genuine complementary roles, particularly cards 1, 5, 6, 7, 15, and 46, unless a small wording improvement is needed.

After editing, review all hybrid bodies alone as one deck. They should sound like a relationship-centered mode with its own purpose, not a second version of handler mode.
