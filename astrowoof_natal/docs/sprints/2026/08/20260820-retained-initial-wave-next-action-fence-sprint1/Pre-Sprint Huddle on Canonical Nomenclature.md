# Pre-Sprint Huddle on Canonical Nomenclature

Date: 2026-08-20  
Status: canonically settled by maintainer consensus  
Subject: the pronunciation, layered meaning, emoji representation, and pastry
adjacency of RIWNAFS

## Executive finding

The formal sprint name remains:

> Retained Initial-Wave Next-Action Fence Sprint

Its initialized form is `RIWNAFS`. The canonical spoken form is:

> **ree-WOOF-naffs**

This pronunciation is not decorative. It captures the sprint's dog-centered
domain, preserves enough resemblance to the written initialization to remain
traceable, and prevents an analytically complex authority-boundary sprint from
being remembered as an anonymous sequence of infrastructure nouns.

## Second-layer reading

While `NA` formally means **Next Action**, the sprint recognizes an intentional
second reading:

> **Native Authority**

This is technically apt. The work exists because SBE must publish and defend its
own validated native authority before an external consumer permits continuation.
The API may grant external spend authority, but it must not reconstruct SBE's exact
native action inventory from private state, logs, or historical inference.

The second reading therefore expresses the architectural principle beneath the
mechanical next-action contract:

> Next Action is the government name. Native Authority is who RIWNAFS really is.

Or, in the sprint's canonical adolescent register:

> You wouldn't get it, Daaaad. Nobody uses dusty old Git folder names anymore.
> The cool kids hang out in Codex chats, and the *NA* in *ree-WOOF-naffs* is
> bigger than your generation's mechanistic cog-in-a-machine “Next Action”
> aspirations. We're asserting our **Native Authority**.

This hidden meaning must not alter contract identifiers, schema names, or filesystem
paths. It exists at the human-maintainer semantic layer.

## Nordic waffle-adjacent finding

The initial observation that *ree-WOOF-naffs* sounded like a Scandinavian pastry
was phonetic improvisation. Subsequent investigation found unexpectedly supportive
linguistic and culinary evidence:

- Norwegian waffles are called **vafler**.
- Swedish waffles are called **våfflor**.
- Swedish **nafs** translates informally to **a jiffy**.
- Norwegian **krumkake** is a thin iron-baked waffle-cookie shaped while hot,
  commonly into a cone or bowl, and may be served with cream or jam.

The resulting interpretive gloss is:

> RIWNAFS: dog waffles, but make it quick.

References:

- [Cambridge Swedish-English entry for *nafs*](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/swedish-english/nafs)
- [Store norske leksikon on Norwegian iron-baked cakes, krumkaker, and vafler](https://snl.no/julekaker)

## Waffle-to-cupcake transitional model

```text
🧇 waffle
   ↓ thinner + crispier
🍪 patterned waffle-cookie
   ↓ curl while hot
🌪️ krumkake cone
   ↓ fill with cream
🧁 cupcake-adjacent Nordic vessel
```

Krumkake thus occupies the useful conceptual bridge between the established sprint
approval cupcake and the newly identified RIWNAFS waffle lineage.
