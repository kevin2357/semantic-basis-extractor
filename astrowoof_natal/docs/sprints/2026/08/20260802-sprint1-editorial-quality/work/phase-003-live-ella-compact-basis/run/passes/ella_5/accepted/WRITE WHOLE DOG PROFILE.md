# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Ella

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Ella is a miniature Schnauzer whose attention is both investigative and relational. She patrols the sensory details of her world, notices changes in people and routines, and often wants her discoveries to matter to the pack. She can be earnest about a household duty, delighted by a fresh game, and surprisingly tender about trust. Her recognizable contradiction is that she is both an independent scout and an involved companion: she wants room to inspect, but she also wants to return with a role, a report, or a performance for someone who appreciates her.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Ella is alert, observant, purposeful, and expressive. She meets the world through a careful first response, often investigating before committing. Her confidence grows when she can make sense of a situation and participate visibly. She is not flatly cautious or uniformly bold; she is a small dog with a serious patrol mind, a lively appetite for novelty, and a strong wish to be recognized as part of the action.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Ella regulates through concrete sensory and relational evidence: familiar places, known sequences, local sounds, scents, and the presence of a trusted person. She may absorb the atmosphere around her and become watchful when the emotional or sensory picture changes. Practical consistency helps her body settle. Warmth matters, but it works best when paired with space, legible cues, and permission to investigate rather than pressure to perform instant calm.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Ella learns readily when information becomes a shared activity. She is motivated by clear cues, repetition with purpose, playful reward, and the feeling that training improves her participation in the pack. Her investigative attention can be shaped into useful coordination through practice. She benefits from short sequences, observable criteria, fair transitions, and opportunities to choose after gathering information.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Ella forms meaningful one-to-one and pack bonds, with affection often expressed through visible participation: greeting, presenting a toy, supervising, checking in, or offering a performance. She may prefer certain people and want acknowledgment from them. Trust is not assumed in every encounter; she gathers information and decides how much access feels safe. Respecting her retreat, investigation, and return path strengthens the relationship more than forcing sociability.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Her rhythm alternates between patrol and play, close observation and sudden movement, serious checking and exuberant release. She enjoys purposeful exploration, new routes, tricks, and small variations on familiar games. Novelty can energize her and even deepen affection when introduced through a trusted relationship. Play is most fluent when success is clear, the atmosphere is warm, and excitement has a planned way back to rest.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Ella's chart repeatedly balances bond with freedom, home-base regulation with outward adventure, and careful investigation with immediate response. She may want to inspect while the household wants to move, seek attention while also needing space, or react quickly before she has finished understanding. These are not character failures. They are workable counterweights: bounded exploration, predictable transitions, meaningful roles, safe distance, and practiced return cues help the impulses cooperate.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Ella's strengths include sensory attention, social observation, persistence, expressive affection, purposeful curiosity, and the ability to turn repetition into skill. Her growth edge is not becoming less herself; it is adding a beat between stimulus and action, learning when an investigation is complete, and distinguishing a useful pack role from self-appointed management. She can develop remarkable coordination when handlers honor her intelligence while keeping expectations simple and consistent.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Ella with warmth, specificity, and affectionate respect. Useful images include a tiny neighborhood correspondent, a household inspector, a scout with a return address, a committee member whose minutes are written in scent, or a performer waiting for the right audience. Her humor comes from disproportion and earnestness: she may assign official importance to a sock, a doorway, a toy presentation, or a couch inspection. Keep the comedy claim-specific and never make her the butt of her own sensitivity.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
Ella is a miniature Schnauzer born August 26, 2015, in Delta, Colorado; use her documented pronouns, she and her. Astrology here is an interpretive writing framework, not a diagnosis or proof of behavior. Ordinary stories must stay within their supplied claim and evidence. Do not invent biography, medical conditions, breed-typical claims, trauma, aggression, exact household routines, or guaranteed reactions. Present observable possibilities rather than certainties, and keep practical advice low-pressure, specific, and non-medical.
