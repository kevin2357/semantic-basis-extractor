# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Ella

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Ella is a perceptive, purposeful Miniature Schnauzer whose world is organized through participation. She notices local details, reads the atmosphere of home, investigates before committing, and often turns attention into a visible pack role. She can be earnest about a bark, playful in a sudden burst, and deeply attached while still needing a private base. Her recognizable pattern is not one fixed mood but a cycle of observing, joining, acting, and returning.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Ella is alert, observant, practical, and socially vivid. She likes to know what is happening and to have a meaningful place in it. Her first response may be concentrated investigation, while her public style can be expressive and supervisory. She is not merely busy: she seeks coherence, usefulness, and a role that lets her vitality be seen.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Home and familiar resting places are important regulators for Ella. She can absorb household tone, pace, scent, and tension, sometimes responding to atmosphere before people identify its cause. Quiet retreat, predictable transitions, and small environmental adjustments help her release intense attention. She benefits from acknowledgment followed by an ending, not dismissal or escalation.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Ella’s attention to scents, sounds, and routines can become a strong training channel. She is motivated by meaningful participation, clear sequences, social connection, play, and rewards that follow completed action. Her learning is best supported by short practice, concrete cues, repetition, and outlets that turn investigation or energy into a useful response.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Ella forms significant bonds and enjoys being noticed, included, and useful to her people. She may seek a favorite person’s attention or perform a household role, then return to a private place. This is a rhythm of attachment rather than inconsistency. Trust grows when she can approach voluntarily, contribute visibly, and retreat without punishment or emotional interpretation.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play can be social performance, pursuit, defense rehearsal, or a way to recruit the pack into shared excitement. Novelty may produce immediate movement or zoomie energy, while adventure is strongest when a secure return route exists. Ella thrives on cycles of launch, engagement, check-in, rest, and renewed choice rather than unbounded stimulation.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Ella’s chart repeatedly balances outward participation with private safety, careful investigation with immediate action, sensitivity with expressive vitality, and serious cue-reading with the need to release information. She may pause and then surge, seek contact and then withdraw, or treat a small signal as important while needing only a small adjustment to settle. These are counterweights to coordinate, not contradictions to eliminate.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Ella’s strengths include observation, scent and cue interpretation, loyalty, social awareness, practical participation, persistence, playfulness, and the capacity to learn usable routines. Growth lies in giving these strengths boundaries: a finish cue for investigation, a safe outlet for action, a protected retreat, and practice that lets her notice without becoming captive to every signal.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Ella as a compact household professional with a serious nose, a vivid public presence, and a surprisingly poetic relationship to rooms and moods. Her humor comes from disproportion: a bark becomes a case file, a cushion adjustment becomes infrastructure, and a play burst becomes a public performance. Keep the warmth affectionate and specific; let her competence and earnestness be funny without making her difficult behavior trivial.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
