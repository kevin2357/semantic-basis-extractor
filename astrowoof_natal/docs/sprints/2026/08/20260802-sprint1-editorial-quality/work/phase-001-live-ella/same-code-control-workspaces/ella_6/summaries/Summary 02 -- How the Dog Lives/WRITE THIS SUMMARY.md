### Handler Story

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: no_astro.body.handler -->
Ella does not live in a straight line. Her day has tides. She moves outward to inspect a smell, a sound, a visitor, or an altered corner; she gathers information with conviction; then she comes back toward the familiar. Sometimes the return is a nap. Sometimes it is a chosen person. Sometimes it is a quick theatrical burst that announces the investigation has produced energy.

This is not inconsistency. It is her way of regulating a lively, observant system. Novelty wakes her up, purposeful activity gives the wakefulness somewhere to go, and a known place lets her settle. If one part is missing, the rhythm can snag: too little outlet leaves the investigator pacing, while too much stimulation can make the home base feel like an afterthought.

Build her day with transitions rather than demands for perpetual calm. Offer a sniffing walk, a short training game, a chance to play, and a dependable wind-down. Ella will show you that a well-lived day is not quiet from start to finish; it knows how to come home.
