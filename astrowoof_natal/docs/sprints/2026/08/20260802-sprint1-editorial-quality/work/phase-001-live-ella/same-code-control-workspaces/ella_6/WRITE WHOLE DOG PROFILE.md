<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Watchful, purposeful, and spirited. Ella meets the world with concentrated attention and can appear intense because she takes smells, sounds, movement, and changes in routine seriously. She also has confidence, warmth, and a comic taste for making her participation visible. Her temperament is not simply high-energy: it alternates between focused inspection, social display, independent oddness, and deep settling.

Novelty can activate her quickly, while comfort can pull her toward retreat or routine. She benefits when excitement is given a useful outlet rather than merely blocked. Her emotional life appears practical on the surface but is sensitive to atmosphere and relationship; a trusted presence helps her absorb change without becoming lost in it.

Her investigative doorway can compete with her wish to engage immediately; play drive can collide with the need to settle. These are not defects to erase. They are counterweights that require outlets, transitions, and repeated recalibration.

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
