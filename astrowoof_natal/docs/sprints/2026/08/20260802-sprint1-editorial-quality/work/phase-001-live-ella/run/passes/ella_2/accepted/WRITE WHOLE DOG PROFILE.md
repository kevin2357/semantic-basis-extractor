### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Ella is a compact, vivid dog with an investigator's attention, a performer's presence, and a strong instinct for returning to what feels like home. She can absorb the atmosphere of a room, inspect its details, join the household with enthusiasm, and then retreat to a chosen base to reset. Her recognizable contradiction is not inconsistency but rhythm: she is both watchful and playful, devoted and self-directed, socially visible and protective of private space. The same dog who patrols a doorway may later conduct a quiet emotional survey from a favorite resting place.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Ella meets life alertly and seriously, with a quick first response that often becomes investigation. She notices changes, follows clues, and prefers to understand a situation rather than pass through it casually. Alongside that concentrated attention is a lively, expressive streak: she can turn play, movement, or a household event into a visible performance. Her temperament is best described as observant, spirited, purposeful, and slightly theatrical, with an oddball independence that keeps her from being entirely predictable.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Ella is sensitive to atmosphere and may absorb the emotional tone, scent memory, and activity level around her. Familiar places and repeated settling rituals help her regulate, but she is not passive about comfort; she may leave safety to investigate or act and then return to re-establish equilibrium. Her emotional life benefits from an order of operations—notice, inspect, move or connect, then settle. Space should be treated as regulation rather than rejection, while stimulation needs a bounded outlet rather than indefinite escalation.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Ella learns best when a rule has a clear shape and enough consistency to investigate. She may pause, examine timing, test a boundary, or repeat a detail before a behavior becomes fluent, but careful learning can become durable skill. Sniffing, local information, patterned cues, short sequences, tricks, and purposeful jobs suit her active mind. She is motivated by useful participation, relationship, novelty with structure, and the satisfaction of mastering a pattern—not by vague pressure or endlessly changing expectations.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Ella forms meaningful one-to-one bonds and may prefer a trusted person as a source of orientation, companionship, and shared regulation. She can mirror a familiar pace or seek contact when stimulated, yet closeness works best when it includes an exit. Her loyalty is compatible with distance: she may remain nearby, choose a private spot, and return when the bond feels inviting rather than compulsory. Trust grows through calm repetition, respect for retreat, voluntary reconnection, and a person whose cues remain clear under changing conditions.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play and adventure are active, visible, and often connected to pack contribution. Ella may chase, inspect, carry, search, perform, or supervise as though each activity has a meaningful role. Novelty can trigger immediate movement or a burst of zooming, but home base remains important as the place from which excursions launch and where they end. Her best rhythm alternates purposeful stimulation with deliberate landing: a clue, a task, a short display, a return, and rest.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Ella's central counterweights are bond and freedom, public participation and private safety, comfort and novelty, investigation and immediate action. She may want her person involved while also wanting to choose the distance; she may want to settle while a new sound demands inspection; she may be tired yet still feel responsible for the household's activity. These tensions should not be flattened into a single label. They become manageable when each need receives a legitimate channel and when transitions are taught rather than forced.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Ella's strengths include perceptiveness, persistence, emotional attunement, expressive play, loyalty, and the ability to turn observation into skilled action. She can become a capable partner when her sensitivity is given structure and her energy a meaningful job. Growth edges include tolerating incomplete information, ending an investigation, shifting from display into rest, and accepting closeness without surrendering autonomy. Her development is supported by fair limits, scent-rich enrichment, short rehearsals, predictable returns, and opportunities to contribute without being responsible for everything.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Ella's voice is imagined as dry, observant, dignified, and faintly managerial: she notices the irregularity, files the report, and then surprises everyone with a burst of play. Humor should arise from her exact priorities—the forensic doorway inspection, the formal patrol, the dramatic volunteerism, or the request for affection at a carefully chosen distance. Useful imagery includes base camp, office hours, weather reports, case files, handoffs, expeditions, and small public performances. Keep the warmth affectionate rather than mocking; her seriousness is part of her charm.
