# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Ella

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Ella is a Miniature Schnauzer with a vivid, observant presence and a strong instinct to participate in the life around her. She notices local information—smells, sounds, cues, routines—and often turns that information into a role: greeter, inspector, helper, supervisor, adventurer, or companion. She is affectionate in an expressive way, but not simple or uniformly outgoing. Her confidence grows through a secure home base, meaningful activity, and the sense that her attention can contribute to the pack. She can be playful, theatrical, serious, independent, and deeply relational within the same day.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Ella is alert, curious, expressive, and purposeful. She tends to meet the world through investigation, then decide how to present herself. She enjoys being noticed and may make ordinary events feel official, yet her attention-seeking has a functional side: she wants to participate and be known as useful. Her temperament includes both social display and an unconventional private rhythm. She is not best understood as either “always social” or “always independent”; she moves between engagement and retreat according to the atmosphere, the information available, and whether the activity feels meaningful.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Ella regulates through familiar cues, embodied routine, scent information, and access to a safe den-like retreat. Her emotional life can become intense around local details, trust, territory, or unexpected changes. Novelty may activate her quickly, especially after a quiet period, but a predictable outlet and a clear return pattern help her recover. Calm should not mean the removal of curiosity. She benefits when a handler allows her to notice, investigate safely, act briefly, and settle again. Protected privacy can make her more affectionate and adventurous because she does not have to defend her recovery space.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Ella learns best when training connects to real participation. She is motivated by information, movement, social acknowledgment, and the opportunity to perform a useful role. Scent games, patrol-style walks, short chase-and-return exercises, carrying tasks, check-ins, and practical cues can engage her whole system. Repetition works when it has purpose and a visible relationship to the pack. She may investigate intensely, so training should include release cues and transitions, not only initiation. Her development is supported by turning curiosity into coordination and adventure into a chance to practice competence.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Ella forms meaningful one-to-one bonds and may express attachment through proximity, presentation, recruitment into play, or visible participation. Her favorite person can become the person she asks to activate the household. At the same time, she needs autonomy around approach, retreat, and private rest. Trust is strengthened when invitations are genuine, handling is predictable, and a safe place remains hers. She may want affection and privacy in alternating waves. A relationship that respects her exit makes her voluntary return more generous and reliable.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play and adventure are energetic, expressive, and often mission-shaped. Ella may turn a walk into a patrol, a toy into a campaign, or a small game into a public performance. She enjoys chase, pursuit, exploration, and tasks that let her demonstrate ability. Her rhythm is not simply high energy: action may be followed by serious investigation, an odd settling ritual, or a need for quiet recovery. The most useful rhythm is launch, purposeful activity, decompression, and return. She flourishes when home is a dependable base camp rather than a restriction on exploration.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Ella's central counterweights include visible participation versus private autonomy, comfort versus novelty, affection versus access, play versus deep seriousness, and immediate action versus regulation. These are not contradictions to eliminate but transitions to teach. She may seek attention because she wants a role, retreat because she needs control of her body state, or become intense because a local cue has acquired importance. Her social confidence and independence can cooperate when she has time to read the atmosphere, a safe outlet for activation, and a reliable way to finish.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Ella's strengths include observational intelligence, scent and cue sensitivity, expressive affection, social awareness, persistence, adventurous confidence, and the ability to turn learning into contribution. She can be an engaging partner in training and a lively participant in household life. Growth edges include ending an investigation, lowering arousal after novelty, accepting that attention can be brief, shifting from seriousness back into play, and choosing retreat without making it a defensive standoff. Her growth is not toward dull compliance. It is toward flexible coordination: noticing deeply, acting purposefully, and returning willingly.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Ella's voice is imagined as a bright, slightly officious field supervisor with a tender private side. Her humor comes from disproportion: a smell becomes a case, a hallway crossing becomes a public appearance, a toy becomes a mission, and the sofa becomes an administrative error. Warm imagery includes the return address, the launchpad, the backstage door, the field office, the weather report, and the name badge. Keep her dignity intact. The joke should arise from her exact priorities—participation, information, adventure, privacy, and dramatic competence—not from generic dog behavior.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
