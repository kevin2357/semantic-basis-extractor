# Whole-Dog Orientation for This Run

Build the private whole-dog orientation described in the handbook from this
run’s `DOG DETAILS.md`, complete `FULL CHART BASIS.md`, candidate fields, and
read-only context.

Consider integrated temperament; emotional life and regulation; learning and
motivation; relationships and trust; play, adventure, and daily rhythm;
tensions and counterweights; strengths and growth edges; suitable warmth,
humor, and imagery; and factual cautions.

Do not return this orientation. Use it to preserve one recognizable dog and to
coordinate the four summaries.

The candidate fields and read-only prose are a partial reader-facing view, not
the complete deck. For ordinary cards, whole-dog understanding supplies
characterization and tone only. It does not add evidence beyond the
corresponding rich claim record and projected-term context.
