# AstroWoof Card and Field Requirements

The Editorial Polish Handbook defines the product experience and editorial
standard. This document defines the concrete reader-facing system.

## Claim deck

The natal deck contains fifty selected claim cards. A card may translate a
direct astrological claim or a synthesis supported by multiple claims. Each
card must remain bounded by its own supplied claim and evidence and should
stand alone when filtered or read out of deck order.

Each claim has nine headline/body renderings formed by three audiences and
three astrology-density levels. They share one supported insight but are
purposeful siblings rather than copied prose with substitutions.

## Audiences

- **Handler** helps the person recognize what may be happening and respond
  usefully. Advice should be observable and specific.
- **Direct to dog** addresses the dog as `you` with dignity, affection,
  reassurance, encouragement, or an honest challenge. It is composed for the
  dog rather than mechanically adapted from handler prose.
- **Hybrid** reveals the reciprocal dog-human situation through a shared scene,
  ritual, misunderstanding, sequence, adjustment, or outcome. Its subject is
  the interaction.

Audience changes purpose, not mandatory grammar.

## Astrology-density levels

- **No astrology** fully translates the insight into ordinary dog life. It
  contains no signs, planets, houses, aspects, angles, nodes, astrology jargon,
  or references to the chart.
- **Light astrology** names and interprets the one or two most useful supplied
  astrological sources, then returns quickly to recognizable behavior.
- **Full astrology** may explain relevant planets, signs, Doghouses, angles,
  nodes, aspects, geometry, operators, and orb strength while remaining warm,
  readable, and evidence-bounded.

## Card-level fields

Each card also has:

- three concrete dos;
- three concrete don’ts;
- one funny dog quote;
- one imperative dog quote;
- one canine joke.

These fields belong to the card as a whole. Quotes should sound speakable in
the dog’s imagined voice. Humor should arise from the exact behavioral
mechanism or tension, not merely from generic dog material or a repeated comic
world.

## Summary cards

The four summaries alone may use the complete selected and unselected chart.
They appear first to users and use the fixed lenses described in the handbook:
Who the Dog Is, How the Dog Lives, What the Dog Needs, and How the Dog Grows.
Each summary uses the same three audiences, three astrology densities, and
card-level supporting fields.

## Reader-facing language

Projected terms and registry definitions are decoding aids. Recover their
compound meaning, then express it through natural astrology and recognizable
dog life. Do not expose source labels, provenance machinery, or the writing
process to the reader.
