# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a socially attuned, bright-hearted Golden Retriever whose curiosity, devotion, and capacity for sudden investigative detours all belong to the same recognizable dog. He is drawn toward shared adventures and meaningful one-to-one connection, yet he retains a notably independent streak whenever a new scent, odd detail, or shift in atmosphere requests his full attention. His best life has both a trustworthy human center and enough room to roam, sniff, play, and participate.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Kevin presents as warm, noticeable, and a little unconventional in his first responses. He is inclined to make connection, to notice the social weather, and to seek a role in the group; at the same time, novelty can make him abruptly singular-minded. He is neither simply compliant nor simply free-spirited. He is a companionable original who wants to understand what is happening before deciding how fully to join it.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin appears porous to mood and atmosphere. He may move closer, offer play, or become absorbed in an investigation when the emotional tone changes. Clear transitions, calm cues, familiar resting places, and short cooperative rituals are likely to help him settle. His intensity is best met with structure and acknowledgment rather than with pressure or abrupt disconnection.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Kevin is likely to learn especially well through repeated, relationship-rich practice. He has strong motivation for action, play, exploration, and rewarding outcomes, but the most satisfying learning sequence gives all of them a place: a clear cue, a successful response, a reward, and sometimes permission to resume sniffing or moving. He benefits from tasks that feel like shared missions rather than abstract tests of obedience.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Trust is a deep organizing principle for Kevin. He may explore confidently when a favored person remains emotionally available, and he may check back not because he lacks courage but because connection strengthens his courage. His social style leans toward harmony, fairness, and a meaningful role alongside his people. Respectful pauses, consistent greetings, and warm recall rituals help him keep both closeness and independence in view.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Kevin's play has momentum and display: he may bring a toy, invite action, or turn a shift in energy into a shared game. Adventure also matters, particularly when it includes safe novelty and time to investigate. His rhythm is often outward movement followed by a relational check-in, then another burst of interest. Bounded sniffing, cue-and-release games, and play followed by calm companionship fit him well.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Kevin can feel pulled between novelty and smooth social presentation, between following an intriguing clue and staying responsive to a close person, and between easy rewards and the wish to keep exploring. These are not flaws or contradictions to erase. They are the places where he needs a practical rhythm: pause, orient, investigate safely, return, and receive acknowledgment. His independence works best when it has a reliable route back to connection.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin's strengths include social sensitivity, wholehearted participation, curiosity, capacity for cooperative learning, and an ability to make play or shared activity feel relational. Growth lies in helping him navigate surprise without losing his orientation to a handler, and in making recalls, arrivals, and transitions rehearsed successes. He is likely to thrive with patient, consistent guidance that gives his big engagement a usable job.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin as a devoted explorer, quiet social diplomat, and occasionally overqualified scent detective. His humor comes from treating small clues as urgent fieldwork, toys as formal proposals, and household moods as matters requiring a well-timed appearance. Use images of elastic connection, weather reports, maps, expedition headquarters, and tiny official assignments. Keep the warmth genuine: he is earnest rather than ridiculous.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
