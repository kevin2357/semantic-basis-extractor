# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a warm, socially attuned Golden Retriever with an explorer’s curiosity and a surprisingly serious relationship to trust. He is likely to turn toward people, play, and possibility, yet he does not move through them casually. He watches the emotional weather, investigates what changes, and thrives when his freedom to explore has a dependable human return point.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Kevin’s core style blends sociability, originality, and conviction. He can be bright and demonstrative with his favorite people, yet his first response may be delightfully unconventional when something unexpected enters the scene. He is not simply easygoing: he is a participatory dog, inclined to make contact, examine the terms, and then join wholeheartedly.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin appears permeable to atmosphere. Tone, tension, excitement, and the shape of a shared moment may register quickly in his body and attention. Regulation is helped by clear transitions, calm voices, familiar rituals, a safe place to settle, and permission to process novelty before being asked for polished behavior. His sensitivity is a strength when it has boundaries.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Kevin learns especially well when training is relational, active, and rewarding. He benefits from short repetitions that connect energetic play to a return cue, and from adventure tasks followed by an easy success. His curiosity is investigative rather than merely restless; offering structured sniffing, choice, and cheerful reconnection gives it a useful channel.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Close companionship matters profoundly to Kevin. He may seek a favorite person’s attention, mirror their emotional tone, and treat trust as something with real weight. A brief independent detour or intense investigation should not be mistaken for lack of attachment. He does best with people who allow space without withdrawing connection and who make reunions warm and predictable.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play, pursuit, and exploration bring Kevin alive, especially when they are shared. He may leap toward novelty, become absorbed in a scent or strange detail, and then be ready for a lively game. His best rhythm alternates outward movement with check-ins, novelty with familiar reward, and sociable activity with a quiet landing place.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Kevin’s chart repeatedly pairs bond with freedom, comfort with expansion, and visible social participation with surprise-driven independence. These are not contradictions to eliminate. He needs a life in which he can investigate safely, receive clear cues, return to connection, and recover from stimulation. Structure works best when it functions as a bridge rather than a restriction.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin’s strengths include emotional responsiveness, enthusiastic participation, deep loyalty, social grace, and a lively capacity for discovery. Growth comes through practicing recovery after surprise, returning during arousal, and learning that a cue can lead back to more fun rather than ending it. His confidence expands when people reward cooperation without suppressing his individuality.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin as a golden-hearted field investigator, an unofficial household diplomat, and an eager adventurer with a nose for emotional weather. His humor should arise from the dignity he grants tiny mysteries, his apparent role as ambience manager, and his conviction that a side quest can coexist with devotion. Keep the voice affectionate, intelligent, and never mocking.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
Do not invent medical, trauma, aggression, rescue-history, or breed-specific claims. Treat chart language as interpretive rather than diagnostic. Observable examples should remain conditional: Kevin may investigate, check in, drift toward atmosphere, or need a pause; they are not reports of documented incidents. Ordinary stories must stay within their own supplied claim and evidence.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
