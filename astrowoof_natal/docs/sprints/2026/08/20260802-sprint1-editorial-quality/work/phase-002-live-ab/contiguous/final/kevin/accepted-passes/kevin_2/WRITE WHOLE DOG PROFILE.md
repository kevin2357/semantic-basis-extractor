# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a warm, socially aware Golden Retriever with a lively outward spark and a surprisingly layered inner compass. He is drawn toward people, play, investigation, and shared adventure, but his confidence works best when he can remain in an understandable relationship with his world. He is not simply easygoing or simply intense: he is a dog whose curiosity, theatrical energy, deep attachment, and need for fair structure continually meet in the same bright, expressive character.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Kevin appears eager, present, and a little delightfully unconventional in his first responses. He is inclined to engage, investigate, and make himself part of the action, with a strong appetite for pursuit, play, and exploration. Social harmony matters to him, yet he benefits from latitude to follow a scent, take a brief independent detour, or decide how to approach something new. His characteristic rhythm is outgoing motion followed by a meaningful check-in.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin is sensitive to atmosphere and may absorb the emotional texture of a moment before his body catches up with a rule or request. Comfort is sensory and relational: a familiar base, a readable environment, calm transitions, and a trusted person all help. He may need small, clear steps when emotionally stirred. This is not a reason to remove boundaries; it is a reason to make boundaries predictable, kindly delivered, and easy to re-enter after a reset.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Kevin is highly teachable when learning feels like a shared activity with a practical or playful purpose. Novelty, scent investigation, changed settings, animated games, and short discovery tasks can activate his interest. He benefits from familiar cues placed inside new but manageable contexts. Clear sequencing matters, as does rewarding reconnection rather than making every cue an end to pleasure. He is likely to learn best through curiosity held inside a secure routine.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Trust is central to Kevin’s organization. He can bond deeply, notice subtle signals, and invest strongly in one-to-one connection as well as broader pack harmony. His attachment is not best understood as passive dependence; it often fuels his willingness to explore, cooperate, and return. He needs people to be legible—fair, steady, and clear about expectations. Gentle rituals around arrivals, departures, changes, and reunions can help his devotion become flexible rather than pressured.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Kevin is an adventure-minded dog whose play drive can turn atmosphere into action quickly. He may enjoy chase, searches, theatrical games, and expeditions that feel shared with his people. His most satisfying rhythm is likely to include a launch into interest, a cooperative check-in, another chance to explore, and a quiet landing afterward. He can be funny in his seriousness about a new object or a moving toy, as though every walk and game has been promoted into an official mission.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Kevin’s major counterweight is between closeness and self-directed movement. He may want to investigate, act, or withdraw briefly while also remaining connected to a favorite person or household group. Another important adjustment lies between emotional permeability and training structure: a rule may need to be simplified during a sensitive moment. His supports are predictable routines, safe exploration, rewarding recalls, accessible retreat space, and transitions that preserve dignity rather than force a choice between freedom and belonging.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin’s strengths include social warmth, imaginative play, strong curiosity, a capacity for deep loyalty, and an ability to turn novelty into learning. Growth comes through practicing flexible transitions: returning without losing adventure, settling without being shut down, and exploring without disconnecting from his person. He benefits from small successful repetitions more than dramatic tests. With patient, fair guidance, his intensity can become devotion, his alertness can become intelligence, and his enthusiasm can become skilled cooperation.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin as an affectionate expedition leader, an enthusiastic inspector, and a dog with a real taste for dramatic assignments. His imagery belongs to trail maps, field reports, house audits, open doorways, soft dens, movie scenes, and little shared rituals. Humor should arise from the grandeur he may lend to a leaf, toy, visitor, shifted chair, or snack negotiation. Keep the voice warm and respectful: Kevin is never foolish, only sincerely committed to his important canine work.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
Avoid inventing biography, trauma, medical issues, aggression, specific household events, or claims about his actual training history. Do not treat chart language as diagnosis or certainty. Golden Retriever breed information is limited to the supplied identification and should not be used to infer behavior. Frame all behavioral descriptions as possibilities supported by the relevant card evidence, and keep individual stories bounded to their stated placements, aspects, or syntheses.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
