# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a warm, socially attuned Golden Retriever with a lively outward curiosity and a surprisingly porous inner weather system. He is drawn to people, shared activity, and the possibility just beyond the familiar, yet he does best when adventure has a recognizable return route: a trusted person, a clear cue, a quiet resting place, or a dependable routine. He reads as an enthusiastic companion rather than a solo operator—an explorer who keeps making the expedition a group project.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Kevin’s temperament combines brightness, sociability, and a mildly unconventional first response to the world. He can arrive with alert, playful purpose, but his visible behavior is rarely simple: novelty, movement, affection, and the wish to understand the moment may all appear together. He is likely to be memorable for his expressive invitations—look here, come along, throw this, confirm the plan—rather than for detached independence.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin appears sensitive to atmosphere and may register a room’s pace in his body. Familiar touch, known scents, a reliable resting place, and a gradual transition from excitement to quiet are likely to help him regulate. His emotional sensitivity should be read generously: a pause, a drift in attention, or a wish for proximity may be a sensible recovery strategy rather than a character defect.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Kevin is likely to learn most deeply through short, repeatable cooperation embedded in daily life. Clear cues, predictable sequences, and warm releases give him a structure in which to practice without feeling shut out. He may enjoy action and novelty, but his strongest training foundation is likely to be the ordinary ritual: check in, wait briefly, participate in care, succeed, and continue with the good thing.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Relationships are central to Kevin’s confidence. He may invest heavily in a close person and benefit from practical rituals that make connection legible—calm departures, reliable reunions, check-ins, and shared routines. This is not a portrait of a dog who must be constantly reassured; it is a portrait of one for whom secure coordination makes loyalty more flexible, playful, and adventurous.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Kevin’s play and adventure impulses are sociable. He is likely to enjoy a toy, a trail, or a new sensory detail most when it can be offered to somebody else as part of a shared moment. His curiosity can become immersive, so the ideal rhythm is not relentless stimulation: allow a pause to investigate, invite him back into contact, then move forward together. He needs both horizons and home base.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Kevin’s chart suggests recurring pulls between action and attachment, novelty and steadiness, public participation and private decompression. He may surge toward the new thing while also checking that his person remains available. The counterweight is not suppression. It is sequence: connection before release, an outlet after waiting, and recovery after a full experience.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin’s strengths include social warmth, a talent for making shared activity feel meaningful, responsiveness to relationship-based practice, and an ability to turn curiosity into invitation. His growth edge is learning that he can explore without having to manage every emotional or relational variable himself. Patient routines, generous recalls, and calm transitions can turn his intensity into confidence and his sensitivity into excellent communication.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin as an optimistic expedition host with a sensitive internal barometer: a dog who may deliver a tennis ball as an official mood report, pause at a breeze as though it contains plot, and treat a familiar cue as a team handshake. The tone should be affectionate, observant, and lightly amused. Use images of base camps, scenic routes, invitations, household rituals, and an eager internal committee; never make him sound foolish for caring deeply.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
