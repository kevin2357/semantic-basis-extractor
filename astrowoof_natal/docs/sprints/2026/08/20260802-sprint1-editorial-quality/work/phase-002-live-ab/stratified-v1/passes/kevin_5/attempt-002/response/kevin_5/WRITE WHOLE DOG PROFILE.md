# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a warm, vivid Golden Retriever whose strongest current runs through relationship: he notices his people, invests deeply, and often turns connection into participation. He is both an eager joiner and an original investigator—ready to greet, play, help, or head toward the next horizon, yet inclined to pause, sniff, assess, or take an unconventional route into a situation. His best life pairs a dependable emotional home base with room for choice, exploration, and a meaningful role in the pack.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Kevin presents as socially bright, expressive, and lightly theatrical, with a distinctive edge that keeps him from being merely predictable. He is likely to meet the world with visible interest, especially where a person, a task, or a new possibility is involved. Beneath the cheerful presence is a serious instinct to determine where he belongs and what he can contribute.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin appears sensitive to atmosphere and may absorb the pace or feeling of a setting before he settles into it. Reliable routines can help him, though he may occasionally need a gentle reset when an emotional moment and an expected rule do not line up. Quiet check-ins, a familiar cue, and permission for brief decompression are likely more useful than pressure when he seems dreamy, hesitant, or overfull.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
He is motivated by shared activity, close attention, novelty, and the satisfaction of having a clear job. Kevin may learn especially well through short sequences that begin with connection and end in play, exploration, or an obvious success. His curiosity can become intensely focused around new information, so training benefits from clear return cues and small, repeatable practices rather than trying to suppress his investigative spark.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Trust is central for Kevin, not decorative. His attachment, confidence, attention, and willingness to accept structure all appear to gather around the question of whether the relationship and situation feel sound. He may show loyalty conspicuously, seek a favorite person’s response, and carefully inspect unexpected social moments. The most helpful handling is calm, consistent, and transparent: acknowledge his check-in, show that the human is managing the situation, and invite rather than force connection.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Kevin’s play drive is sociable and immediate; attention from a loved person can readily become a request for action. He also has a strong appetite for adventure, scent stories, changing routes, and the odd surprise that demands investigation. A good daily rhythm alternates purposeful play, safe exploration, tiny training games, and genuine rest. He is likely to enjoy feeling like an active companion on the expedition, not merely a passenger.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Kevin repeatedly balances bond with freedom. He may want to be included, helpful, and close, while also needing a little agency over his pace, direction, or investigation. He also balances firm routines with a sensitive body and emotional atmosphere. These are not flaws to eliminate: a stable base, bounded choices, and clear transitions let both sides cooperate instead of compete.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin’s strengths include visible warmth, strong loyalty, readiness for shared play, original curiosity, and a natural ability to make a relationship feel alive. His growth edge is learning that pauses, returns, and calm cues do not reduce adventure or belonging; they make both more reliable. He will likely flourish with handlers who turn his intensity into useful tasks, let curiosity have safe outlets, and reward the moment he chooses to reconnect.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin as an earnest, golden-hearted expedition partner with a small internal operations center, a serious sniff-reporting habit, and a tendency to treat ordinary household transitions as meaningful events. His humor comes from the disproportionate professionalism he brings to toys, visitors, departures, and new smells. Keep the warmth affectionate rather than mocking: Kevin is not chaotic for its own sake; he is a loyal participant trying to understand the assignment and improve the adventure.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->

### Factual Cautions

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
The chart supports interpretive tendencies, not claims about observed history, diagnosis, aggression, medical needs, or fixed behavior. Do not invent specific incidents, traumas, household members, training history, or reactions to strangers. Breed is provided as factual identity but should not be used to add unsupported breed stereotypes. In individual stories, remain inside that story’s supplied evidence rather than importing unrelated chart material.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.factual_cautions -->
