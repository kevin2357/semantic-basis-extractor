# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a warm, vivid Golden Retriever whose life is organized around two simultaneous impulses: join the pack wholeheartedly and keep enough freedom to investigate the world in his own surprising way. He is socially attentive, physically expressive, and often eager to turn ordinary events into shared activity. Trust runs deep for him; so do curiosity, play, and the wish to have a meaningful place in the household. He can be affectionate and adventurous, watchful and oddball, eager to cooperate yet liable to pause when a signal or relationship feels unclear.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Kevin is friendly, noticeable, and purposefully involved. He tends to meet the world with an unusual first move, then organize himself through observation, play, and contact with trusted people. He may present as a greeter, escort, helper, supervisor, or enthusiastic expedition leader. His temperament combines Golden Retriever warmth with a distinct appetite for novelty and a serious concern for the quality of close bonds.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin absorbs atmosphere readily and can be affected by changes in tone, routine, or household cohesion. Familiar people, safe places, and predictable sequences help him regulate his body and attention. Deep trust may make him intensely loyal and sensitive to relational shifts. When overstimulated or uncertain, he benefits from a calm retreat, clear transitions, and time to process rather than pressure to perform sociability.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Kevin learns most readily when practice is relational, playful, and connected to daily care. He responds to clear sequences, fair limits, scent-based investigation, small successes, and rewards that feel emotionally secure. Repetition is not inherently dull for him when it teaches a useful way to participate in pack life. His growth comes through turning enthusiasm and impulse into dependable cooperation without flattening his curiosity.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Kevin forms strong one-to-one bonds and may monitor the balance of important relationships closely. He mirrors trusted companions, seeks meaningful participation, and often expresses affection through shared activity. Trust is both a source of courage and a vulnerable center: it lets him explore, but disruption can make him inspect, guard, or hold close. Respectful invitations, consistency, and room to choose help him experience closeness as secure rather than constraining.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
His natural rhythm alternates between investigation, display, shared adventure, and settling into a secure base. Kevin enjoys walks with new information, playful tricks, safe novelty, and activities that let him contribute visibly. He may range outward, check back, and set off again. After excitement, a familiar settling ritual helps him convert stimulation into contentment.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Kevin's chart repeatedly sets freedom beside bond, novelty beside routine, and strong impulse beside the need for a usable outlet. He may want to explore while also needing a return address; seek attention while needing a clear ending; investigate deeply while being asked to move on. These are not contradictions to eliminate. They are coordinating forces that work best when the handler provides structure without needless restriction.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin's strengths include loyalty, social intelligence, optimism, embodied enthusiasm, pattern recognition, trainability, and the ability to make participation feel joyful. He can become a capable helper or visible pack-role performer when given a defined lane. His growth edges are tolerating uncertainty, releasing a task or investigation, accepting limits without personalizing them, and learning that rest does not cancel belonging.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin as a golden, attentive character with a nose for hidden information and a tendency to appoint himself to household office. His humor comes from disproportion: a doorway becomes a checkpoint, a walk becomes an expedition, and a small trick becomes a ceremonial performance. Keep the warmth affectionate rather than mocking. Useful imagery includes return addresses, maps, committees, job descriptions, patrols, shared treaties, and a dog who believes every ordinary day deserves an itinerary.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
