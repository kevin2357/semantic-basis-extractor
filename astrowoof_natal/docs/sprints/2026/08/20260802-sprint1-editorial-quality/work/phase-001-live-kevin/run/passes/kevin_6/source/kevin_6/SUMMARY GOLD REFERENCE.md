## Summary 1: Who He Is

### Do 1

### Do 2

### Do 3

### Don't 1

Do not label every unconventional response as stubbornness before checking what information caught his attention.

### Don't 2

Do not make affection unpredictable as a way to force compliance; clarity builds more trust than emotional leverage.

### Don't 3

Do not confuse sociability with shallowness or assume a quick recovery means the interaction did not matter.
