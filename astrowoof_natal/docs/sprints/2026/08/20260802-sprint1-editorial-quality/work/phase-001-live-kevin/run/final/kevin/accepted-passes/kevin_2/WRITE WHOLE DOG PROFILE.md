# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a warm, vivid Golden Retriever whose social enthusiasm comes with a surprising amount of self-direction. He meets the world energetically, often through display, investigation, or play, yet he also needs a dependable private base from which to absorb what is happening. His strongest relationships organize his confidence: he can range outward, experiment, and perform when the bond offers a reliable return address. He is not simply outgoing or sensitive; he is a dog whose curiosity, loyalty, intensity, and need for choice repeatedly meet in the same moment.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
Kevin is friendly, expressive, curious, and unusually alert to the pattern of an interaction. He may greet with enthusiasm, turn a routine into a performance, or investigate an unexpected detail with total concentration. Alongside that sociable brightness is an oddball streak: he may change direction, revise the game, or choose an unconventional first response. His temperament is best understood as engaged but self-authored.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin absorbs atmosphere readily and may register changes in voice, rhythm, household activity, or relational tone through his body and behavior. Familiar spaces help him settle and process, though retreat need not mean withdrawal; he may be listening from his safe base. He benefits from calm transitions, protected rest, and predictable repair after disruption. Deep trust gives feelings weight, so consistency is regulating rather than merely convenient.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
Kevin learns well when curiosity has a job. Novel objects, routes, scents, and changing circumstances can interrupt him, but they can also provide the hook for investigation, search, and practice. He responds best to short, meaningful sessions that alternate exploration with familiar return cues. Clear routines, humane limits, and opportunities to choose help convert energetic impulse into developable coordination.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Kevin forms vivid one-to-one bonds and may orient strongly toward a favorite human or familiar companion. He wants connection without feeling captured, so a relationship works best when welcome is steady and participation has some room for choice. Trust is a central organizing domain for him: it affects identity, attention, rules, confidence, and willingness to explore. Fairness, follow-through, and patient repair are especially important.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
Play and adventure are major channels for Kevin's vitality. He can turn a scent, breeze, new path, or encouraging look into pursuit, display, or a comic expedition. He enjoys expanding his radius but often benefits from check-ins and a clear return ritual. His ideal rhythm includes outward investigation, purposeful movement, shared engagement, and an off-duty period in which he can settle without being recruited back into supervision.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Kevin repeatedly balances bond with freedom, public participation with private recovery, novelty with routine, and emotional permeability with impulse control. He may want attention and autonomy simultaneously, or understand a rule while needing more time to feel settled enough to perform it. These tensions are not evidence of a divided character. They are recurring coordination tasks, made easier by choices, clear boundaries, safe outlets, and reliable returns.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
Kevin's strengths include social warmth, expressive play, investigative attention, loyalty, adventure optimism, and the ability to make trust operational. He can turn sensory or emotional impressions into action and can learn from novelty when it is structured. His growth edges involve pacing, reorientation, tolerating changed terms, and distinguishing a useful investigation from an escalating detour. He grows through practice that preserves dignity and gives energy a specific destination.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin as an affectionate field character: bright-eyed, slightly theatrical, emotionally perceptive, and capable of treating a small household event as a major expedition. Humor should come from his exact contradictions—an eager host who needs an office break, an explorer who wants a return address, an investigator who audits every cone. Useful imagery includes listening posts, control rooms, trail markers, weather, contracts, and shift schedules. Keep the warmth generous; the joke should never make sensitivity seem foolish.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
