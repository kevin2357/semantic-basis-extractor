# Whole-Dog Authoring Profile: Kevin

Complete this profile first, using `DOG DETAILS.md` and the entire `FULL CHART BASIS.md`. This becomes the shared characterization for every story in the workspace.

### Integrated Portrait

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->
Kevin is a warm, socially vivid Golden Retriever whose curiosity and companionship tend to arrive together. He meets the world with a memorable mixture of optimism, investigative attention, and occasional oddball timing. He wants to explore, play, and participate, but his freedom is rarely emotionally solitary: a trusted person, shared purpose, or dependable return point helps his confidence expand. He is both an affectionate audience-seeker and an adventurous pack member, with a large-hearted presence that can turn ordinary routines into events.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.integrated_portrait -->

### Core Temperament

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->
His temperament is buoyant, expressive, curious, and socially oriented. Kevin may approach life with a bright first response—sometimes polished, sometimes unconventional—and he often makes his presence known through a look, lean, toy, performance, or invitation. He is not simply easygoing or simply intense; his friendliness includes investigation, initiative, and a wish to have his role recognized.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.core_temperament -->

### Emotional Life and Regulation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->
Kevin appears sensitive to atmosphere and may absorb the emotional tone of a room or outing. Familiar people, clear routines, and trusted cues help him regulate the movement between excitement and rest. He benefits from permission to observe before acting and from boundaries that do not shame his sensitivity. His emotional steadiness grows when he can notice, reconnect, and then choose a manageable next step.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.emotional_regulation -->

### Learning and Motivation

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->
He is motivated by participation, praise, movement, and the chance to perform a meaningful task. Training works best when it is concrete, brief, and relational: a clear cue, an observable behavior, precise reinforcement, and a clean release. Novelty may initially produce investigation or exuberance, but trust and repetition can turn that first response into learning. Kevin’s energy is not an obstacle to education; it is useful fuel when given a route.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.learning_motivation -->

### Relationships and Trust

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->
Kevin forms vivid one-to-one bonds and may have a strong preference for a favorite human or familiar companion. He seeks recognition through active bids rather than quiet attachment alone, yet he can also offer gentle, atmospheric companionship. Trust is deep and behaviorally important: a dependable person and consistent cue can organize his confidence, play, and exploration. He should be invited into relationship without being made responsible for managing everyone’s feelings.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.relationships_trust -->

### Play, Adventure, and Daily Rhythm

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->
His natural rhythm alternates outward expansion with social return. He may investigate a new trail, chase a toy, or launch into play, then look back, rejoin, or ask his person to participate. Adventure is most satisfying when it is shared, and play is most organizing when it includes a practiced pause or ending. He enjoys a little theater, but he also needs recovery rituals that make settling part of the game.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.play_adventure_rhythm -->

### Tensions and Counterweights

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->
Kevin carries a recurring tension between immediate action and relational orientation, between unusual first responses and the wish to be understood, and between absorbing atmosphere and moving confidently into the world. He may appear restless when he actually needs a role, or clingy when he is asking for active partnership. Conversely, his optimism can make him look more self-sufficient than he feels. The counterweight is not suppression: give his drive structure, his sensitivity time, and his bonds clear boundaries.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.tensions_counterweights -->

### Strengths and Growth Edges

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->
His strengths include warmth, social initiative, curiosity, expressive communication, enthusiasm for practice, and the ability to make trust operational. He can transform novelty into skill when a handler supplies a familiar bridge. Growth edges include impulse control, tolerating delayed attention, translating idiosyncratic greetings, and learning to stop while still successful. He is likely to flourish when training preserves his dignity and gives him useful ways to contribute.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.strengths_growth -->

### Voice, Warmth, Humor, and Imagery

<!-- BEGIN FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
Write Kevin as a golden, bright-bodied character with a nose for atmosphere and a talent for turning small moments into expeditions. His humor comes from disproportion and sincerity: a puddle receives a committee review, a toy becomes a formal letter, and a five-minute routine becomes opening night. Keep the warmth affectionate rather than mocking. Images of trails, thresholds, stages, weather, return points, and shared missions fit him, provided each story uses its own shape.
<!-- END FIELD: profile.voice_humor_imagery -->
