## Critic findings

The critic returned six findings.

### Upstream reconception

1. Four aspect/synthesis cards repeatedly tell the same reader-facing story:
   Kevin launches independently or responds unusually, then reconnects through
   a trusted person. Different chart evidence does not produce sufficiently
   different situations, stakes, or conclusions.
2. The four summaries use their nominal lenses but repeatedly converge on one
   solution: preserve curiosity and intensity, provide a clear cue or bounded
   outlet, and ensure a dependable return to safety or relationship.

The critic correctly refused to treat either problem as a few-field rewrite.

### Locally repairable

3. Investigation, reports, case files, customs, and bureaucracy form one
   overused comic engine across unrelated cards.
4. Handler bodies repeatedly deny a negative interpretation before presenting
   the affirmative behavioral insight.
5. Several headlines promise the same independence/connection story without
   naming whether the card concerns greeting, play, interruption, or transition.
6. Two full-astro bodies spend too much space re-enumerating the same Libra
   Doghouse-8 concentration and trust-through-structure conclusion.

The cap selected findings 3 and 4 across eight paths in six card locations.
Findings 5 and 6 remained diagnosis-only.

## Candidate outcome

The Luna/low editor changed seven paths and preserved one. It remained
structurally valid and mechanically non-worsening with zero lint warnings.

Strong changes include:

- `I cannot nap until I have checked the revisions` becoming `One more sniff…
  there. This room feels nap-shaped again`;
- bureaucratic expedition approval becoming physical anticipatory adventure
  humor;
- handler bodies leading with observable sequence or decision point instead of
  reflexive reassurance against refusal, distance, or defiance.

The omitted handler body already began with a concrete offbeat greeting scene,
so preservation was appropriate.

## Mixed edit

`I am not wandering off. I am establishing dramatic context` became `I found
the interesting part. Now watch me make my entrance.` The replacement escapes
the recurring administrative/investigative mechanism, but the original has
more comic snap. This is useful counterevidence against automatic promotion:
mechanical validity and diagnosis alignment do not guarantee that every local
replacement is aesthetically superior.

## Conclusions

- The critic is perceptive enough to add information beyond deterministic QA.
- Its upstream/local classification is more valuable than indiscriminate polish.
- Sparse keep-or-replace editing remains conservative and cheap.
- Qualitative candidates must remain separate until reviewed.
- Upstream critic findings should feed future authoring-plan improvements rather
  than granting the local editor broader authority.
