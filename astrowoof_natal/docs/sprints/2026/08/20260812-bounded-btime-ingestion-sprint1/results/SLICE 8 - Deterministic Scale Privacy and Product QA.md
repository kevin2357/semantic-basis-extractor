# Slice 8 - Deterministic, Scale, Privacy, and Product QA

The bounded semantic product now has a compact, reproducible qualification path
from admitted invariant family through exactly-fifty selection, private claim deck,
provider-minimized packet, deterministic fake authoring, and final QA. Its report
records semantic identities, product topology, observed time and memory, and an
explicit statement that numeric selection values are editorial utility—not

Reversing context order, every object and relationship array, and projected-term

A synthetic 300-member relationship family remains one independent support unit.
It cannot increase the selection beyond fifty or manufacture additional family
coverage. The automated case passed deliberately loose regression guards of ten
seconds and 256 MiB traced peak memory. These are defect tripwires based on observed
execution, not released latency or memory guarantees.

## Capability reductions and fail-closed behavior

Terrestrial-frame `inconclusive` and optional-object `unavailable` dispositions do
not create candidates or claims and do not enter provider-visible material.
Non-invariant candidates, missing dependencies, unknown/mixed foundational policy,
insufficient invariant bases, unsupported selection size, missing terms/evidence,
mixed exact/bounded input, contract/profile/context/source drift, and corrupted
runtime/resource identities remain machine-rejected by the combined bounded suite.

An input that cannot support fifty invariant claims stops explicitly. SBE does not
fill the deck with variable, unavailable, representative-time, or otherwise weaker
material.

## Privacy corpus

The adversarial corpus seeds handler identity, birth date and datetime, earliest and
latest interval endpoints, timezone, latitude, longitude, location evidence, raw
evidence excerpt, workspace path, API key, and authorization header. All are absent
from initial authoring, creative retry when exercised, polish, critic, qualitative
candidate, and structured event payloads.

The dog's display name is deliberately visible because it is an explicitly
documented editorial provider field. This is not reported as a privacy failure or
silently treated as protected.
