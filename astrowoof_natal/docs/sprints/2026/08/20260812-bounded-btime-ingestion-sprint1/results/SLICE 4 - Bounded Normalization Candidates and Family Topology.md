# Slice 4 - Bounded Normalization, Candidates, and Family Topology

## Result

SBE now turns one admitted four-context bounded-Natal family into a separate,
deterministic `astrowoof.bounded_natal.candidate_policy.v1` pool. It normalizes
objects and relationships by upstream `correspondence_id`, retains every context
record, and refuses any projected row whose epistemic classification is not
`invariant`.

The pool distinguishes:

- foundational Natal objects;
- individualized direct-aspect and declination relationships;
- family-collapsed derived objects and relationships;
- dependency-only coordinate-transform topology; and
- initial invariant configurations composed from multiple relationship-family
  units between the same canonical root owners.

Every non-configuration candidate retains source references, correspondence IDs,
all four projected context records, proof scope, projected-term references,
direct and prerequisite evidence references, unresolved opaque prerequisites,
root-owner identity, evidence-family identity, independence groups, and
record-independence groups where upstream supplies them. Configuration membership
refers to candidate-family IDs rather than raw sibling rows.

`family_accounting` states the raw correspondence count, independent support-unit
count, and `raw_record_count_is_weight: false`. Adding another raw member to an
existing evidence family changes its traceable membership but creates neither a
new candidate nor a new support unit.

The configurable foundational policy supports the approved
`strong_preference` default, `mandatory_when_available`, and `portfolio_neutral`.
At this slice it assigns an explicit policy status only; numeric editorial utility
and portfolio selection remain Slice 5 work.

## Anti-precision and disposition boundary

Candidate authority contains no upstream orb/range evidence, structural-strength
field, confidence field, representative state, or reconstructed scalar. Relationship
`projection_relevance_score` remains present only inside the preserved SPC context
record and retains its upstream target-relevance meaning; Slice 4 does not consume
it as certainty, strength, or weight.

The private `astrowoof.bounded_natal.disposition_report.v1` accounts for every
projected row, every materialized source-evidence record, every upstream
outside-declared-scope ID, and the complete upstream source-feature disposition
map. Conditional, variable, unavailable, and inconclusive evidence can be retained
as non-authored lineage/disposition but cannot become a projected candidate.
