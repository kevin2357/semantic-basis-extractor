# Slice 5 - Bounded Editorial Utility and Exactly-Fifty Portfolio

## Result

SBE now selects an exactly fifty-claim, invariant, dependency-closed bounded-Natal
portfolio under `astrowoof.bounded_natal.editorial_utility.v1`. The utility is an
SBE editorial objective only. It is not confidence, probability, epistemic
strength, structural strength, astrological strength, or a representative-time
estimate.

The versioned objective records normalized components for foundational salience,
ordinary-material preference, family-collapsed centrality, SPC-declared target
relevance, distinctiveness, topology/configuration value, narrative yield, context
completeness, compression, and incremental coverage. It separately records
redundancy, evidence-family saturation, root-owner derived-family saturation, and
dependency-cost penalties. Component and penalty weights are published in every
selection audit.

SPC relationship relevance is consumed only when the row declares
`evidence_family_equal_allocation` and `raw_record_count_is_weight: false`.
Allocated siblings are summed once within the already collapsed candidate family;
tests prove one full allocation and ten one-tenth allocations contribute the same
target relevance.

The optimizer evaluates complete candidate closure and charges configurations the
average value of their full missing bundle. A configuration therefore cannot gain
an advantage merely by pulling many prerequisites into the portfolio. Derived
families receive diminishing returns around common root owners, while distinct
derived material and closed configurations remain eligible.

## Portfolio and failure contract

- The v1 size is exactly 50 and cannot be overridden.
- Every selected record retains `epistemic_classification: invariant`.
- Every authored candidate dependency is selected; dependency-only transform
  topology remains retained without becoming prose authority.
- Candidate IDs are unique, editorial tiers are closed to `foundational`,
  `primary`, `supporting`, and `supplemental`, and every selected/rejected decision
  carries an auditable reason and bounded utility breakdown.
- A pool or closure unable to produce fifty claims fails with the machine-readable
  `insufficient_invariant_basis` code. It never pads with variable, unsupported,
  duplicate, or fabricated claims.
- Missing/cyclic dependencies, mixed epistemic authority, duplicate IDs,
  unsupported relevance accounting, and non-v1 sizes fail closed.

The selection pass updates a copy of the complete bounded disposition report with
selected, retained-dependency, redundant, displaced, selected-lineage, and
unselected-lineage classifications. It does not discard the Slice 4 source and
outside-scope accounting.
